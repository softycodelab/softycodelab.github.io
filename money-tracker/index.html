What you can do

Log it in seconds

Type the amount, pick a category, save. Account, date and note are optional.

Accounts and credit cards

Cash, bank and card balances; statements, due dates and remaining instalments.

Plan

Recurring transactions such as rent, bills and subscriptions; shopping lists with prices.

Analysis

See where your money goes, category by category and month by month.

Your data and your Google account

You do not need an account to use the app; all your records are stored on your device. A Google account is only requested for the optional cloud backup.

  • You turn on the cloud backup. If you do not, the app never connects to your Google account.
  • The backup is written, encrypted, to a hidden folder in your Google Drive that belongs only to this app.
  • The app sees only its own backup files; it cannot access your other files in Drive.
  • Backups are never sent to us or to any other server. You can remove the account from the app at any time.
