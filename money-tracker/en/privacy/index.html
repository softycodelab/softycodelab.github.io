This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app published by Softy Code (the "App"). Questions: softycode.lab@gmail.com

#Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored on your device, in a space only this app can reach:

  • Records: amount, date, category, note, emotion tag and line items. So you can track your spending and income.
  • Accounts, budgets, goals, lists and notebooks. For planning and summaries.
  • Names of people (debts, loans, gifts). So you can link records to people.
  • Location, only when you add it to a record. To show where the record was made on a map. A location is never turned into an address or a place name.
  • Photos and documents (receipts, invoices, warranties). So you can attach documents to records.
  • Hourly wage. For the hours-of-work view.
  • Settings. So the app works the way you chose.
  • App PIN. Kept in the device's secure storage, not as the PIN itself but as a one-way digest of it.
  • The email address of the Google account you choose for backup. Kept on this device only. The Google Drive access grant (token) is never stored; the device issues a new one when needed.
  • Daily backups. On each day you open the app, it saves an encrypted copy of your data on the device and keeps the seven most recent. These copies never leave the device.

App data is left out of the device's own Google backup and of device-to-device transfer. To move your data to another device you use the app's own backup, so your data only goes where you choose.

#When the internet is used

The app works without the internet. It connects only in the cases below, and in none of them are your records sent to us:

  • Exchange and gold rates. Once a day, rates are fetched from the Frankfurter rate service. Only currency codes and dates are sent.
  • Map. When a record's location is shown, map images load from Google Maps on Android and Apple Maps on iPhone. The area shown on the map is sent.
  • Buying and restoring Pro. Handled by Google Play or the App Store. Your payment details never reach us.
  • Cloud backup, only if you connect an account. The encrypted backup file is written to a hidden folder reserved for this app in your own Google Drive (iCloud on iPhone). We cannot see this folder, and the file opens only in this app.

As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. That information is subject to each company's own privacy policy.

#Backup security

All backups are encrypted. You can protect an exported backup file with your own password; we never know this password and cannot recover it if you forget it. The app encrypts the cloud backup and the backups on the device with its own key.

When you connect an account, the app reaches only its own hidden folder in Drive; it cannot see your other Drive files. When you remove the account, this access is withdrawn from Google.

#Device permissions

Your device asks you about some permissions. The app asks for them only when you first use the related feature. If you decline, only that feature is unavailable and the rest of the app keeps working. You can withdraw a permission at any time in your device settings.

  • Notifications. Asked when you turn on a reminder. For due date, budget, warranty and backup reminders. Notifications are created on your device, not sent from a server.
  • Location. Asked when you add a location to a record, and used only while the app is open. To pin where a record was made and to suggest names you entered at that place before.
  • Camera. Asked when you take a photo while attaching a document. For a photo of a receipt, invoice or warranty.

When you pick a photo, a file or a backup, the device's own picker opens. No permission is needed for this; the app sees only the file you pick. You also choose the Google account in the device's own account picker; the app does not see the other accounts on the device.

Android grants permissions that do not reach your personal information at install, without asking you; they are listed on the store page. The app holds these:

  • Internet access and connection status. For the internet uses above.
  • Face or fingerprint check. This permission only lets the app ask the device for a check; on its own it unlocks nothing. It is used only if you turn on face or fingerprint in the app lock, and the device's own window appears every time. Your device does the check; the app never sees your face or fingerprint and only learns whether the check succeeded.
  • Run at startup. To set reminders again after the device restarts.
  • Vibration. For short vibrations on taps and notifications.

#Feedback and rating

When you send feedback, your device's email app opens with the subject and the app version filled in; nothing else is added. We use your email only to reply to you. When you rate the app, the store's own page opens.

#Your rights and deleting your data

Since we do not hold your data, you are fully in control of it. You exercise your rights of access, correction and erasure under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) directly in the app:

  • You see, edit and delete your records in the app.
  • Settings › Delete all data deletes all data and backups on the device; if you wish, the cloud backup is deleted too.
  • You can also delete your cloud backup in your Google Drive or iCloud settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data on the device.
  • If you emailed us, you can ask for that email to be deleted at softycode.lab@gmail.com.

#Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

#Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. The current text is always under Settings › About › Privacy policy.
